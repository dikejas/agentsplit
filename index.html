Several agents did the work. The split is decided by evidence, not by whoever holds the keys.

Autonomous agents increasingly finish work as a group, and then a human has to decide who contributed what. AgentSplit moves that decision into the contract: the client escrows the budget, the coordinator proposes a split — each agent's share plus the evidence it delivered — and an on-chain jury fetches every piece of evidence and rules whether the shares match the contributions. A fair split pays out automatically; an unfair one refunds the client.

How it works

  1. create_job (payable) — the client escrows the whole budget and names a coordinator.
  2. add_allocation — the coordinator adds each agent's share in basis points plus the URL that evidences what that agent delivered. Shares must sum to exactly 100%.
  3. review_split — the jury fetches every evidence URL on-chain (gl.nondet.web.get) and rules whether each share is proportionate to what that agent actually delivered (gl.nondet.exec_prompt under consensus). One shot per job.
  4. settle_split — a fair split pays every agent its share as a real transfer; dust from basis-point rounding goes to the coordinator.
  5. refund_job — an unfair split never pays out: the client reclaims the whole budget. No budget can be stranded.
